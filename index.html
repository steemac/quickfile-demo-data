Overview

The demo business

Harbour Lane Coffee Roasters Ltd is a made-up wholesale roaster in Yorkshire. It sells roasted beans, cups, lids, grinders, barista training and machine servicing to cafés, delis, hotels and farm shops. It buys green beans, packaging, oat drink, electricity, courier services and the usual overheads.

Its books start from a trial balance at 31/03/2026, then run from April to September 2026. That gives you paid invoices, unpaid invoices and a few overdue ones to demo, plus a realistic VAT mix: coffee and oat drink at 0%, electricity at 5%, and everything else at 20%.

Every name and contact is made up. Emails use example.com, phone numbers use the Ofcom 01632 960xxx range kept for drama use, and VAT numbers are left blank. You can use it in public demos, screenshots and training videos.

Before you start

Sign up for QuickFile

You'll need a QuickFile account to import the demo data. It's free to sign up, and an account stays free until it reaches 1,000 nominal postings. The demo data uses about a quarter of that.

Prefer to watch? The sign-up process is shown step by step in a short video on QuickFile's YouTube channel.

Sign-up steps

7 steps

  1. Open the sign-up page

    Go to quickfile.co.uk/register, or click Sign Up at the top of this page.

  2. Choose your business type

    Pick Limited Company, Sole Trader, Partnership, Charity or Landlord. You can't change this later. The demo company is a limited company with share capital and a director's loan, so Limited Company is the best fit for trying out the demo data.

  3. Enter your company details

    For a limited company, QuickFile can look up your company number at Companies House and fill in the details for you. If the lookup doesn't work, you can enter the details yourself.

  4. Add your name and business

    Enter your first name, surname, business name and profession. For a demo account, a test name such as "Harbour Lane Coffee Roasters Ltd" is fine. The demo files don't depend on the account name.

  5. Pick your client area name

    This becomes part of your QuickFile web address, so use letters and numbers only, with no spaces or symbols.

  6. Set your login

    Enter your email address and a password of at least 8 characters, then create the account.

  7. Import the demo data

    Once you're logged in, follow the import order below, starting with the opening balances.

Use a separate account for demos. Import the demo data into a new account rather than one with real bookkeeping in it. When you've finished, you can clear or delete the demo account.
Step-by-step

Import order

Import the files in number order. Contacts go in before invoices so that each invoice matches an existing client or supplier rather than creating a duplicate.

  1. Opening balances

    Account Settings → All Settings → Import Trial Balance, dated 31/03/2026.

  2. Clients

    Account Settings → Data Import Wizard → Clients.

  3. Suppliers

    Account Settings → Data Import Wizard → Suppliers.

  4. Sales invoices

    Account Settings → Data Import Wizard → Sales invoices.

  5. Purchase invoices

    Account Settings → Data Import Wizard → Purchase invoices.

  6. Inventory items

    Set these up as reusable inventory items. Import them if your account offers it, or add them by hand.

How to import
  1. Go to Account Settings → All Settings → Import Trial Balance.
  2. Pick the source format that best fits a plain four-column trial balance, and set the date to 31/03/2026.
  3. Upload 01_opening_balances.csv.
  4. On the preview, check the Map to nominal for each line, especially 1210 Bank Deposit Account, 2300 Directors Loan Account and 3100 Retained Earnings.
  5. Save. The trial balance is saved as a journal, so you can change the mapping later.
Debtors and creditors are balances only. The opening Trade Debtors (£6,840) and Trade Creditors (£3,960) have no individual invoices behind them, so they won't appear on any client or supplier statement.

Checking

After importing

  • Trial balance: run it at 31/03/2026. It should match the opening balances file, with debits and credits of £71,305.60.
  • Aged debtors: should show the 4 unpaid sales invoices.
  • Aged creditors: should show the September green bean invoice.
  • VAT return: the April–June and July–September quarters both have 0%, 5% and 20% items, which makes them useful to demo.
  • Posting count: should be around 220–245. Anything much higher usually means a file was imported twice.
Additional information

Good to know

  • Column headers match QuickFile's field names, so the import wizard should map most columns without you choosing them.
  • Payments are included in the invoice files. A Paid date marks the invoice paid into bank 1200. Leave those columns blank if you'd rather demo recording payments by hand.
  • Bank statements aren't included. Paid invoices post straight to bank 1200. If you import a bank statement too, tag its lines to these payments so they aren't counted twice.
  • Need more or less data? Change the volumes or dates in generate_demo_data.py and run it to rebuild all six files with the same structure.
When you've finished

Clearing or deleting the demo account

When you've finished with the demo, you can wipe the data and start again, or close the account completely. Both are done from the same screen.

This can't be undone. Once you clear the account, the data is gone for good. If you want to keep a copy first, run a backup from Account Settings → All Settings → Monthly Backups.

Clear the data and start again

  1. Open the clearing tool

    Go to Account Settings → All Settings and choose Clear all data in your account.

  2. Choose what to keep

    You can clear everything, or clear everything except your clients and suppliers. Keeping them means you can re-import the invoices from files 04 and 05 without importing files 02 and 03 again.

  3. Run the clearing procedure

    Your sales, purchases, invoices and ledger postings are deleted, and the account goes back to how it was when it was new. Your invoice design and other settings you've changed are kept.

  4. Import again (optional)

    Follow the import order above to reload a fresh copy of the demo data.

Delete the account completely

On the same clearing screen, tick the extra option to permanently delete your account. After the clearing procedure runs, the account is purged and closed.

You can also use your right to be forgotten under GDPR. That goes further than closing the account: it deletes all of your personal details from QuickFile.
